Is it ready for iPhone Duo?

We read the App Store release notes of popular apps every day to see which ones have been optimized for the inner display.

    How we check

    Apple started accepting apps built for iPhone Duo on October 5, 2026. Only apps built with the iOS 27.1 SDK or later can fill the inner display. Older apps still run, but with black bars on the sides (MacRumors). Every app gets one of three statuses:

    Optimized
    The developer’s release notes say the app is optimized for, supports or was redesigned for iPhone Duo. We quote their exact words.
    Not confirmed
    The app has been updated since October 5, but the release notes don’t say it’s optimized. Most apps only need a rebuild to fill the inner display, and many developers don’t mention it, so some of these will already work full screen.
    Not optimized
    The app hasn’t been updated since October 5, so it can’t have been built for iPhone Duo yet.

    The apps come from the US App Store’s top free, paid and top grossing charts in every category, plus App Store searches for iPhone Duo and foldable apps. Once we find an app, we keep checking it, even after it drops off the charts. For each app updated since October 5, we read every release since then, not just the latest one.
